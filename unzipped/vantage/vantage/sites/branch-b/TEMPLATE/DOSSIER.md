# Cluster Research Dossier (filled at proposal time)

Scout completes this before proposing the cluster. Incomplete dossier -> the
Decision Queue item is auto-rejected by the SEO/AEO Editor's sanity check.

## 1. Opportunity
- Search demand evidence (queries + estimated volume, marked as estimates)
- Competition snapshot: who ranks, how strong
- Why now (trend, new product, program, gap)

## 2. Monetisation
- Affiliate program(s): name, network, SA-affiliate eligibility confirmed?
- Commission rate + cookie window
- Fallback if the program rejects the application

## 3. Test plan
- 3-5 article slugs (buyer-intent first)
- What the win looks like at week 4-6 (target clicks/conversions)

## 4. Honest risks
- Seasonality / fragility / policy risk (e.g. YMYL topics - avoid)
